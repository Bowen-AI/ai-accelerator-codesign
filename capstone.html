Final project · Design review

A workload-driven accelerator architecture recommendation.

The final assessment is a reproducible technical argument: identify a bottleneck, compare alternative hardware responses and recommend an implementation direction with clearly stated limitations.

Project brief

The design study may use a synthetic Transformer layer, a publicly specified benchmark or another openly documented AI workload. It should involve at least two contrasting operating conditions—for example, small-batch decoding and large-sequence prefill.

01 · Baseline

Characterize

Provide operation counts, estimated memory traffic, latency bounds and assumptions about mapping and utilization.

02 · Alternatives

Experiment

Compare compute, memory and combined architectural changes, including sensitivity to workload and system assumptions.

03 · Recommendation

Justify

Identify the preferred trade-off, what remains uncertain and which higher-fidelity validation should follow.

Deliverables

Design memo

A concise technical report with the workload definition, baseline, alternative designs, calculation methods, numerical results, limitations and recommendation.

  • Workload dimensions, precision and resource demand
  • Measured versus modeled quantities clearly distinguished
  • At least four design scenarios
  • Sensitivity to utilization and data-movement assumptions
  • Validation plan for higher-fidelity implementation

Five-slide design review

  1. Workload and problem statement
  2. Baseline bottleneck
  3. Architectural options
  4. Experimental results and uncertainty
  5. Recommendation and proposed next experiment

Keep all examples public or synthetic; the project does not require employer-internal performance measurements.

Evaluation criteria

DimensionStrong submission
Analytical qualityAccurate workload accounting, consistent units and assumptions, reproducible calculations.
Architectural insightTrade-offs tied directly to identified bottlenecks rather than isolated peak specifications.
Experimental rigorControlled comparisons, sensitivity analysis and meaningful counterexamples.
Implementation awarenessClear separation between idealized models and unmeasured effects including routing, clocking, power and area.
CommunicationWell-structured, decision-relevant evidence and a practical next-stage validation plan.
Modeling limitation. The provided roofline simulator estimates only compute and off-chip memory latency floors; it does not estimate actual silicon area, energy, power or clock frequency. Such claims require external tools or empirical measurements, with documented assumptions.
